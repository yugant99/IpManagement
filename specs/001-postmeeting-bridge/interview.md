a planning guide, not permission to ask a dependent question early. Ask every currently
unblocked decision together, recommend an answer, then wait. Do not force equal-size batches.

or acceptance decision; preserve a short change record. Already asked IDs never renumber.

A contradiction is resolved within its existing question record before it counts answered.

RFP/FR IDs. Ask the minimum clarification needed; do not fabricate an extra counted decision.

bounded research agents. No paid model routing is executed by this interview.
