## Presenter and operator checks

Before presenting, know which snapshot is loaded, which saved run is selected and whether a button computes from current stored evidence or advances the synthetic feed. “Reconcile stored inventory” does not advance the scenario clock; “Run now” acquires the next cycle. Do not enable the timer or click past the agreed cycle plan.

Restore only a dedicated demo copy, with its service stopped and its prior state retained. Initial and final evidence snapshots have different purposes. Never reset or migrate the retained original Stage 5 acceptance store. If a supported operation is refused, show the concrete error and refresh its reviewed versions; do not bypass controls for the presentation.

A separate agent's procedural walkthrough demonstrates reproducibility on this Mac. It does not establish human presenter fluency, recipient training or portable-host acceptance. Spencer retains Part 6 packaging and recipient/operator delivery. A missing registered package is a visibility gap, not a claim about work elsewhere.

## Questions and precise answers

- **Is this live discovery?** No. Known synthetic sources feed real local reconciliation and saved results.
- **Are policies configurable?** The acquisition schedule is configurable. The displayed rule thresholds are fixed; no general policy/workflow editor is implemented.
- **Did approval fix the network?** Approved registration changed intended inventory locally. Reconciliation determines the evidence outcome. No live network action occurred.
- **Is a closed case the same as healthy evidence?** No. Latest evidence and operational closure are separate; recurrence can reopen the case and renew notification.
- **How much is complete?** Use the separate [15-scenario catalog](DEMO_CATALOG.md), historical 16 Stage 5 cases and [111-row questionnaire](QUESTIONNAIRE_ROW_MAP.md). Mixed evidence classes are not one completion percentage. The [customer Phase 1/2 gaps](PHASE_COVERAGE.md) remain.
